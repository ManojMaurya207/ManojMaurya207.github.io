Request Account Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Asian Gaskets account and associated personal information by contacting us using the email address below.

Privacy & Account Deletion

privacy@asiangaskettradingcorporation.in

How to Submit Your Request

  1. Send an email to privacy@asiangaskettradingcorporation.in.
  2. Use the subject line Account Deletion Request - Asian Gaskets.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Asian Gaskets account.
  4. Confirm that you want your account permanently deleted.

What Happens After Your Request

We will verify the request to ensure it is legitimate and then process deletion of the applicable account information associated with your Asian Gaskets account.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You may need to create a new account if you want to use the App again.

Data Stored on Your Device

Deleting your account does not automatically remove locally cached application data from your Android device. To remove locally stored app data, clear the App's storage and cache through Android Settings or uninstall the Asian Gaskets app.
