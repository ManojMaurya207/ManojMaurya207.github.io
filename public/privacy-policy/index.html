Privacy Policy

Asian Gaskets Android App

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Asian Gaskets ("we," "our," or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Asian Gaskets Android application, a professional B2B platform for automotive gasket catalog management and visualization.

Asian Gaskets is developed by MADS Development and is designed for engineers, procurement officers, and workshop managers who require secure access to gasket diagrams and technical specifications.

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account in the Asian Gaskets app, we collect the following personal information:

  • Email Address: Required for account creation, login, and authentication purposes.
  • Password: Used for secure account access. Your password is encrypted using industry-standard cryptographic hashing and is never stored in plain text or accessible to us.
  • Display Name: Optional field that you can provide to personalize your profile within the app.
  • User ID: A unique identifier automatically generated by our authentication system to link your account data.

Technical Information

The App may temporarily store the following technical data for functionality purposes:

  • Image Cache: Catalog images (gasket diagrams, vehicle thumbnails, brand logos) are cached locally on your device to improve performance and enable faster loading. This cache is stored in your device's temporary cache directory and does not contain any personal information.
  • Catalog Cache: Brand, vehicle, and gasket catalog data is temporarily stored in memory while you use the app. This data is not personal information and is cleared when you close the app.

Authentication Data

We use Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google LLC, to manage user accounts securely. When you log in or sign up, your email address, password, and optional display name are transmitted securely to Firebase's servers for authentication purposes.

Information We Do Not Collect

Asian Gaskets is committed to minimal data collection. We explicitly do not collect, access, or transmit the following types of information:

  • Location Data: We do not access or collect your device's GPS location or any location-based information.
  • Camera or Media Files: The app does not access your device's camera, photos, videos, or other media files.
  • Device Identifiers: We do not collect device-specific identifiers such as IMEI, MAC address, or Android Advertising ID.
  • Contacts or SMS: We do not access your contacts, call logs, or text messages.
  • Microphone or Audio: The app does not record audio or access your device's microphone.
  • Bluetooth or USB Devices: We do not access Bluetooth or USB connected devices.
  • User-Generated Content: The app does not allow users to upload files, images, documents, or other content.
  • Usage Analytics or Tracking: We do not use analytics tools, tracking pixels, or behavioral monitoring systems.

Note: All catalog data (brands, vehicles, gasket diagrams, and part specifications) is pre-packaged within the app and loaded from the device's local storage. The app operates in an offline-first mode and does not connect to external servers or APIs to fetch catalog content.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Authentication and Account Management: To create your user account, authenticate your login, and maintain your session within the app.
  • Security: To protect your account from unauthorized access and ensure the security of our authentication system.
  • App Functionality: To provide you with access to the gasket catalog, vehicle information, and interactive diagram viewing features.
  • Performance Optimization: To cache catalog images locally on your device, reducing load times and improving user experience.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, analytics, or sharing with third parties for commercial purposes.

Data Storage and Retention

Cloud Storage

Your account information (email address, display name, and encrypted password) is stored securely on Firebase Authentication servers, which are operated by Google LLC. Firebase employs industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest. For more information about Firebase's security practices, please visit Firebase Privacy and Security.

Local Storage on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your Android device:

  • Image Cache: Stored in the app's cache directory (cacheDir/image_cache). This cache is automatically managed by the system and may be cleared when storage space is needed or when you clear the app's cache in Android settings.
  • In-Memory Cache: Catalog data (brands, vehicles, gaskets) is temporarily stored in memory while the app is running and is automatically cleared when the app is closed.

Important: We do not store your email address, password, display name, or any personal information on your device. All personal account data resides exclusively on Firebase servers.

Data Retention

Your account information is retained for as long as your account remains active. If you request account deletion (see "Your Rights and Data Deletion" below), your personal information will be permanently deleted from Firebase servers.

Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement appropriate technical measures to protect it:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between the app and Firebase servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols.
  • Password Protection: Your password is hashed using cryptographic algorithms and is never stored or transmitted in plain text.
  • Screenshot Protection: The gasket diagram viewer screen is protected with Android's FLAG_SECURE feature, which prevents screenshots and screen recording to protect proprietary technical diagrams.
  • Firebase Security: Firebase Authentication employs multiple layers of security including secure infrastructure, regular security audits, and compliance with industry standards.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

Third-Party Services

Asian Gaskets uses Firebase Authentication, a third-party service provided by Google LLC, for user authentication and account management.

Firebase Authentication

  • Purpose: To securely manage user accounts, authenticate logins, and maintain session state.
  • Data Shared: Your email address, encrypted password, optional display name, and a unique user identifier (UID) are transmitted to and stored on Firebase servers.
  • Privacy Policy: Firebase is operated by Google LLC. For more information about how Google processes data, please review the Firebase Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Important: Asian Gaskets does not use the following Firebase or Google services:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore or Realtime Database
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring
  • Google Analytics
  • Google Ads or AdMob

Other Third-Party Libraries

The app uses the following open-source libraries for technical functionality. These libraries do not collect, transmit, or process any personal information:

  • Coil Image Loader: An open-source library used to load and cache catalog images locally on your device. Coil does not communicate with external servers or collect user data.
  • Koin: A dependency injection framework used internally for app architecture. Koin operates entirely on your device and does not transmit data.
  • AndroidX/Jetpack Libraries: Google's official Android development libraries used for UI and system functionality. These libraries operate locally on your device.

Device Permissions

Asian Gaskets requires the following Android permission to function:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): This permission is automatically added by the Firebase Authentication library and is required for the app to communicate with Firebase servers during login, signup, and authentication state verification. This is the only network communication performed by the app.

The app does not request or require any other permissions, including but not limited to:

  • Camera
  • Location (GPS, network-based, or coarse location)
  • Storage or media access (read/write external storage)
  • Contacts or phone
  • Microphone or audio recording
  • Bluetooth or USB
  • Notifications (push notifications are not used)

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.

Your personal information (email address, display name, and user ID) is shared only with Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) for the purpose of account management and authentication, as described in the "Third-Party Services" section above.

We may disclose your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Safety and Security: To protect the rights, property, or safety of MADS Development, our users, or the public, as required or permitted by law.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity, who will be required to honor this Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Asian Gaskets is a professional B2B application designed for adult users in industrial and automotive sectors. The app is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address below, and we will take steps to delete such information from our systems.

Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can view your email address and display name within the app's profile section at any time.
  • Correction: You can update your display name within the app's profile section. To change your email address, please contact us.
  • Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal information.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request permanent deletion of your account and all personal information associated with it:

  1. Send an email to privacy@asiangaskettradingcorporation.in
  2. Include the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Asian Gaskets"
  3. In the email body, include:
    • The email address associated with your Asian Gaskets account
    • A statement confirming that you wish to permanently delete your account

Upon receiving your deletion request, we will:

  • Verify your identity to ensure the request is legitimate
  • Permanently delete your account information (email, display name, user ID) from Firebase Authentication servers
  • Send you a confirmation email once the deletion is complete

Please note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will need to create a new account if you wish to use the app again in the future.

Important: Deleting your account does not automatically clear cached images stored on your device. To remove all app data from your device, you should uninstall the Asian Gaskets app or clear the app's storage and cache from your device's Android Settings.
